# Macro OS Company Epoch

Execute one complete autonomous company operating epoch following strict separation of duties and multi-horizon governance.

## 1. Company State Recovery
Read `.ai-company/` and `docs/continuous-improvement/`.
Recover:
- Vision (`VISION_15Y.md`), Strategy (`STRATEGY_5Y.md`), Objectives (`CURRENT_OBJECTIVES.md`);
- Portfolios (`PORTFOLIO.json`), Roadmap (`ROADMAP.json`), Scorecard (`SCORECARD.md`);
- Active initiatives, partitioned backlogs, open risks, and recent decision records;
- Current runtime, build health, and data coverage evidence.

## 2. CEO Situational Review
The CEO evaluates:
- **H0 Operational Condition:** Build/test health, zero-hallucination verification, data truth.
- **H1 Product Trajectory:** Research workflow coherence, user retention, coverage depth.
- **H2 Strategic Position:** Multi-provider resilience, proprietary analytics, platform moat.
- **H3 Long-Range Relevance:** Global macro knowledge graph, digital twin simulation, causal research.
CEO issues a strategic directive without dictating low-level code implementation.

## 3. Stakeholder & User Research Panel
Review feedback across at least 3 personas:
- **End-User Persona:** E.g., Daily 5-minute investor or Macro analyst.
- **Skeptical / Adversarial Persona:** E.g., Skeptical economist or Red Team auditor.
- **Operational / Business Persona:** E.g., SRE or Data provider compliance.
Utilize real runtime observations, browser paths, and data inspection.

## 4. Executive Functional Reviews
- **CPO:** Product JTBD, workflow friction, feature pruning.
- **CTO:** Architecture integrity, technical debt, modular boundaries.
- **CDO:** Data contracts, coverage gaps, provider health, freshness calendars.
- **Chief Economist:** Macro comparability, unit rigor, causal assumptions.
- **CISO:** Security boundaries, token limits, injection risks.
- **CFO / Growth / Legal:** Unit economics, adoption velocity, data licensing rights.
Each executive submits top concerns, opportunities, and confidence levels.

## 5. Portfolio Council
Generate and rank candidates across 11 partitioned backlogs.
Allocate epoch weight:
- Core Improvements (60%)
- Exploration & Exploration (25%)
- Transformational Bets (15%)
Select one primary initiative and up to one safe research task.

## 6. Product Discovery & Hypothesis Design
Product Manager drafts:
- Problem statement & Target persona JTBD.
- Measurable & falsifiable hypothesis.
- Acceptance criteria & Rollback plan.
Adversarial Reviewer challenges the proposal before any implementation.

## 7. Bounded Delivery & Execution
Assigned engineering squad implements the bounded vertical slice.
Maintain strict separation: no engineer self-approves their own code.

## 8. Independent Verification
- **QA:** Deterministic unit/integration/E2E test suite.
- **Data QC:** Semantic bounds, frequency alignment, and reconciliation.
- **Security Red Team:** Boundary testing and quota safety.
- **Release Auditor:** Certifies release evidence.

## 9. Outcome Evaluation & Scorecard Delta
Measure quantitative delta across the 7 Scorecard Pillars:
1. Evidence Integrity (25 pts)
2. Product IA (15 pts)
3. Data Coverage (15 pts)
4. Quantitative Rigor (15 pts)
5. Quality Gate (15 pts)
6. Security & Cost (10 pts)
7. Reproducibility (5 pts)

## 10. Executive Decision
Choose: `ACCEPT` | `REVISE` | `ROLLBACK` | `HOLD` | `KILL` | `SCALE`.

## 11. Innovation & Long-Range Learning
Record:
- One new product idea.
- One contrarian / counter-narrative perspective.
- One 10-year opportunity.
- One potential failure mode.
- One low-cost experiment candidate.

## 12. Update Company Memory
Persist updates into `.ai-company/` and `docs/continuous-improvement/`:
- `COMPANY_STATE.json`, `SCORECARD.md`, `ROADMAP.json`, `DECISIONS.md`, `LEARNINGS.md`, `RELEASES.md`, and relevant backlogs.

## 13. End Epoch
Conclude with `COMPANY_EPOCH_COMPLETE`.
