# Macro OS — 15-Year Vision (2026–2041)

## Strategic Destination: The Global Macroeconomic Operating System

Macro OS evolves beyond dashboards and chart terminals into the fundamental operating system for global economic intelligence, decision-making, and macroeconomic simulation.

---

## The Four Strategic Horizons

### Horizon 0: Zero-Hallucination Foundation (0–30 Days)
- 100% truthful data contracts across all primary screens.
- Strict route parameter handling (`/countries/:code`, `/compare?series=...`).
- Zero-defect quality gate (`tsc --noEmit`, ESLint zero-warnings, 100% test pass).
- Fail-closed state representation for unhydrated series.

### Horizon 1: The Institutional Core (1–12 Months)
- Comprehensive coverage across G20, ASEAN-6, and frontier emerging markets.
- Universal Compare & Cross-Asset Overlay Engine with lead/lag optimization.
- Multi-mandate Central Bank intelligence (Fed, ECB, BOJ, PBOC, SBV).
- Frictionless research workspaces with cryptographic provenance and URL state portability.

### Horizon 2: Global Macro Knowledge Graph & Simulation (1–5 Years)
- Machine-readable causal macro transmission graphs.
- Real-time nowcasting with alternative high-frequency data (satellite, supply chain, freight).
- Policy transmission simulation ("What if Fed cuts 50bps while PBOC eases?").
- Institutional research collaboration network with reproducible citation lineage.

### Horizon 3: Autonomous Economic Digital Twin (5–15 Years)
- Personalized Economic Digital Twins for sovereign funds, corporations, and individual portfolios.
- Continuous automated hypothesis generation and empirical falsification.
- Causal counterfactual macroeconomic engine with multi-agent economic simulation.
- Global standard research infrastructure replacing legacy financial terminals.
