# Macro OS — Customer Segments & Personas

## 1. Professional Macro & Strategy Analysts (Core ICP)
* **Goal:** Model global policy shifts, run cross-country regressions, export verifiable charts into research notes.
* **Pain Point:** Spending hours manually downloading CSVs from FRED/World Bank and aligning dates in Excel.

## 2. Institutional Portfolio Managers & Hedge Funds
* **Goal:** Detect macro regime transitions, stress-test allocations against historical crises, monitor central bank net liquidity.
* **Pain Point:** Lack of transparent provenance and fear of AI hallucinated narratives.

## 3. High-Net-Worth & Active Retail Investors (The 5-Minute Investor)
* **Goal:** Understand the macro big picture without drowning in financial jargon.
* **Pain Point:** Conflicting media sensationalism and inability to verify if inflation or rate cuts are actually trending.

## 4. Vietnam & Emerging Market Specialists
* **Goal:** Track SBV policy rates, credit growth, VND exchange rates, and domestic real estate liquidity alongside global Fed drivers.
* **Pain Point:** Fragmented domestic data sources and lack of global liquidity bridge.
