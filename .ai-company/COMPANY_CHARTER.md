# Macro OS Autonomous Product Company — Company Charter

## 1. Company Mission
Build and operate Macro OS as the world's most trustworthy, rigorous, and actionable macroeconomic intelligence and research operating system.

## 2. Core Invariants & Operating Philosophy
1. **Truth Precedes Analytics:** Simulated, estimated, or unverified data must never be presented as actual verified observations.
2. **Separation of Duties:**
   - Idea Proposer $\ne$ Approver
   - Code Implementer $\ne$ Code Reviewer
   - Model Builder $\ne$ Model Verifier
   - KPI Owner $\ne$ KPI Scorer
   - Data Producer $\ne$ Data Validator
   - Narrative Writer $\ne$ Evidence Auditor
3. **Fail-Closed by Design:** Unhydrated, unverified, or broken upstream data paths fail closed (honest `--` and unverified state) rather than fabricating plausible filler.
4. **Reproducibility:** Every chart, overlay, correlation, and research thesis must be uniquely addressable via URL state or persistent workspace snapshot.
5. **No False Maturity Claims:** Internal capability maturity tiers (Tier 1 to Tier 5) must be certified by evidence, not marketing aspirations.

## 3. Organizational Structure
- **Tầng 1: Board of Directors** (Chair, Product Board, Data Board, Risk Board)
- **Tầng 2: Executive Office** (CEO, CPO, CTO, CDO, Chief Economist, Chief Research Officer, Chief Design Officer, CISO, CFO, Growth, Legal, Customer Success)
- **Tầng 3: Stakeholder Council & User Research Panel** (5-minute Investor, Macro Analyst, Skeptical Economist, Portfolio Manager, Global Data Challenger)
- **Tầng 4: Product & Engineering Organization** (Frontend, Backend, Data Engineering, Analytics, AI Engineering, SRE)
- **Tầng 5: QA, QC & Adversarial Red Team** (Deterministic QA, Data QC, Security Red Team, Adversarial Reviewer, Release Auditor)
- **Tầng 6: Innovation Office** (Core 60%, Adjacent 25%, Transformational 15%)
