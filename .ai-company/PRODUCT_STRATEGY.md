# Macro OS — Product Strategy & JTBD Framework

## 1. Primary Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)

### Job 1: Global Macro Morning Briefing (Speed & Signal)
* **When:** Morning market opening (8:00 AM).
* **I want to:** Instantly see what key economic releases changed, identify emerging tail risks, and review liquidity conditions across major central banks in under 5 minutes.
* **So that I can:** Position portfolios and anticipate cross-asset market volatility before the open.

### Job 2: Cross-Asset Macro Hypothesis Testing (Deep Research)
* **When:** Formulating medium-term investment or macro research theses.
* **I want to:** Overlay time-series across countries, standardize units with Z-Scores, adjust lead/lag horizons, and calculate correlation coefficients.
* **So that I can:** Scientifically validate lead-lag transmission without spreadsheet errors.

### Job 3: Central Bank & Monetary Policy Tracking (Policy Transmission)
* **When:** FOMC, ECB, or SBV rate decision cycles.
* **I want to:** Review CME FedWatch probabilities, track Fed Net Liquidity changes, and evaluate policy rate trajectory against inflation targets.
* **So that I can:** Assess whether monetary policy is restrictive, neutral, or accommodative.

### Job 4: Historical Crisis Analogy & Regime Comparison (Pattern Recognition)
* **When:** Experiencing acute economic volatility, yield curve inversions, or liquidity contractions.
* **I want to:** Replay historical crises (2008 GFC, 2020 COVID, 1970s Stagflation) in time-locked simulation and measure current similarity.
* **So that I can:** Avoid historical blind spots and stress-test risk scenarios.
